A family-run room

Mum cooks. The rest of us look after you.

Kassem's is a family restaurant in the plain sense: one family, one kitchen, one room. The cooking comes from Mum, the welcome comes from the rest of us, and the place feels personal the moment you walk in — because it is.

People who find us call it a hidden local gem. We would rather you didn't have to hunt: we're on Waterhouse Street, right opposite the Water Gardens.
