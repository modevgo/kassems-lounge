The room

One family. One kitchen. One room.

Kassem's is run by one family, and it cooks the way a family kitchen does: slowly. The tagines take their time. The mechoui is a whole lamb shoulder, roasted until it falls. Kofta and grills come off the charcoal, and the bread is fresh.

It is one room on Waterhouse Street, opposite the Water Gardens. Reviewers keep reaching for the same words: a hidden local gem.
